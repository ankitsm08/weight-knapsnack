About Knapsnack

Made with and a little Indian jugaad

Hey there

I'm Ank, a STEM student and home workout enthusiast who got tired of guessing how much my backpack full of bottles actually weighed. So, instead of buying fancy adjustable weights, I decided to make my own smart solution.

What is Knapsnack?

Knapsnack is a lightweight web app that helps you calculate the best bottle combination to hit your target workout weight. Whether you're training with backpacks, water bottles, or whatever you can find at home, this tool gives you the most efficient way to load up without overthinking it.

Why Knapsnack?

  • Built for home workout lovers who don't have access to a full gym.
  • Inspired by classic Indian jugaad, making the best out of what you have.
  • Because sometimes, a bag full of bottles is the only dumbbell you need.
